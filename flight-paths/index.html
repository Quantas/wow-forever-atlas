Questions

Does WoW Forever change the flight paths?
The Classic network stays, plus six new flight points found in the beta client: three in the Riverglades, two on Mount Hyjal and one in Dun Morogh. They are datamined, not announced by Blizzard, so they may still change.
Are there flying mounts in WoW Forever?
No. Flight masters are the only way to fly.
Do I need to discover a flight path first?
Yes. You have to reach each flight master once before you can fly to it.
