Made to feel like the game

I build the addons I want to play with. They follow three rules.

It looks like Blizzard made it

The game's own frames, fonts, icons and colours. If you can tell where the game ends and the addon begins, it is not finished.

It lives where you already look

On the world map, next to your quests, in the game's options. No extra window to move around or keep track of.

It saves time, it doesn't play for you

Quality of life only. The addons tell you what you need to know and set things up, then the game is yours.

Travelcraft

Plan any trip from the world map

Open the world map, pick where you want to go, and see how long it takes and which way to go. The same planner as this site, inside the game.

  • Routes on the real roads from the Travel panel on the world map, with the time each step takes.
  • Tracked like a quest next to your objectives, with a flight timer for every flight.
  • Every way to travel on the map flight masters, harbours, zeppelins and an arrow at each way out of a zone.
  • Dungeons for your level how far each one is and how safe the way there is.
  • Zone levels on the continent maps, coloured like quests for your character.

SameSame

Your main's setup on every new character

Save your main's profile once. The first time you log in with a new character, one click sets it up the same way, and you are ready to play.

  • Action bars every slot. Spells not learned yet keep their place and appear when you learn them.
  • Key bindings and macros addon key bindings included.
  • Edit Mode layout where your bars, frames and windows sit.
  • Chat windows and interface options tabs, colours, loot, nameplates, camera, scale.
  • Enabled addons the same addons are turned on.

What it can't copy: the settings of your other addons. Each addon keeps them in its own way, so SameSame turns the same addons on, but they keep their own settings.
